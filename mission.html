Resilience in need

🌧️

South Korea's 2025 Floods

South Korea's 2025 floods claimed at least 23 lives and forced over 18,000 people to evacuate. This exposed the growing impact of extreme rainfall, urban flooding, and the danger of impermeable surfaces. Green infrastructure can help absorb rainwater and build more resilient communities.

Source: Wikipedia, 2025 South Korea floods

💧

Vulnerable Elders & Communities

In Seoul, low-income residents living in semi-basement (banjiha) homes face a much higher risk of flooding during heavy rainfall. Many elders live in low-income housing as well, increasing health risks and evacuation difficulties.

Sources: OECD, UN ESCAP, Reuters, WHO, UNDP

We use art to translate engineering concepts into messages of hope, delivering comfort and safety tips to the vulnerable. Rather than simply raising awareness, we transform scientific research into art to communicate dignity and empathy.
