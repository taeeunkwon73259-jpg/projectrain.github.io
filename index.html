Why This Matters

🌧️

Urban Flooding

Flash floods caused by impermeable city surfaces are becoming more severe. We raise awareness about green infrastructure like rain gardens.

👵🏼

Vulnerable Elders

Environmental disasters disproportionately affect the elderly. Our campaign focuses on "Environmental Justice" by protecting those at greatest risk.

🎨

Art as Connection

We use digital art to translate complex engineering concepts into messages of hope, delivering comfort and safety tips to nursing homes.

The Resilience Gallery

Digital artworks depicting nature-based solutions to flooding (like rain gardens and permeable surfaces). Available as printable cards.
